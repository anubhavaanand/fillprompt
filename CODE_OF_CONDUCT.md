# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep discussion about the work: code, content and how the site behaves.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Do not share other people's private information.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space connected to the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to jupiter.mplayplay@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block anyone who breaks these rules.
